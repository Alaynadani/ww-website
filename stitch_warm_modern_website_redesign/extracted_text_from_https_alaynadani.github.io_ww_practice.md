## Commitment to Mutual Respect

To provide an unhurried, exceptional restorative space for each guest, we adhere to the following practice policies. Please review them prior to scheduling. 

event_busy

### 24-Hour Notice

Advance Notice

24 hour advance notice is required when cancelling an appointment. This allows the opportunity for someone else to schedule an appointment. If you are unable to give us 24 hours advance notice you will be charged the full amount of your appointment. This amount must be paid prior to your next scheduled appointment. 

notifications_active Allows waitlisted guests time to receive care

person_off

### No-Show Policy

Missed Session

Anyone who either forgets or consciously chooses to forgo their appointment for whatever reason will be considered a “no-show.” They will be charged for their “missed” appointment. 

shield_with_heart Protects reserved professional time

### Late Arrival

Time Consideration

If you arrive late, your session may be shortened in order to accommodate others whose appointments follow yours. Depending upon how late you arrive, your therapist will then determine if there is enough time remaining to start a treatment. Regardless of the length of the treatment actually given, you will be responsible for the “full” session.
