A little about your therapist

Meet Your Massage Therapist

Hello, my wildflowers! I'm the licensed massage therapist behind Wildflower Wellness Holistic Therapy, and I'm passionate about helping people find relief from pain, reconnect with their bodies, and feel more at home in themselves.

My journey into massage therapy began with a desire to help others heal and regain strength. After discovering the holistic world of massage and experiencing firsthand how profoundly it can help someone living with pain, I knew I'd found my calling. Today, my primary specialties are pain management and trigger point therapy, with additional certifications in craniosacral therapy and infant massage.

I believe every body is different, and every session should reflect that. My approach combines therapeutic knowledge with compassionate, individualized care, creating space for your body to relax, respond, and work toward greater comfort and well-being.

Outside the treatment room, you'll often find me spending time with my sisters and family, trying a new recipe, canning something homemade, or planning my next adventure. I believe wellness extends beyond the massage table and into the little choices we make every day.

At Wildflower Wellness, my goal is to help you feel heard, supported, and cared for—one session at a time. I'm so glad you're here, and I look forward to being part of your wellness journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Not sure what to expect? We're here to help!

Whether you're visiting for the first time or exploring a new massage technique, we want you to feel comfortable, informed, and confident in your care.

How do I know which massage is right for me?expand_more

Every body is different, and so are your wellness needs! Whether you're looking for deep relaxation, relief from muscle tension, or more specialized care, we're happy to help you find the massage that best fits your goals. Feel free to reach out if you're unsure which service to choose.

What is the difference between deep tissue and relaxation massage?expand_more

Relaxation massage uses gentle, flowing techniques to help ease everyday stress and promote a sense of calm. Deep tissue massage focuses on deeper layers of muscle and connective tissue to address persistent tension and tightness. Both can be customized to your comfort level.

What should I expect during my first appointment?expand_more

Your comfort is our priority! We'll begin by discussing your health history, areas of concern, and goals for your session. This helps us personalize your massage experience and ensure you feel comfortable throughout your visit.

Is prenatal massage safe during all trimesters of pregnancy?expand_more

Prenatal massage can be a wonderful way to ease pregnancy-related discomfort and encourage relaxation. Sessions are adapted to your stage of pregnancy and individual needs. Certain pregnancy complications may require approval from your healthcare provider before receiving massage.

What is infant massage, and how can it benefit my baby?expand_more

Infant massage uses gentle, nurturing touch to encourage bonding, relaxation, and comfort. It can be a beautiful way for parents and caregivers to connect with their little ones while learning soothing techniques.

What are craniosacral therapy and myofascial release?expand_more

Craniosacral therapy is a gentle, hands-on approach that encourages deep relaxation, supports the body's natural sense of balance, and promotes overall well-being through light, intentional touch. Myofascial release uses slow, sustained pressure and gentle stretching to ease tension within the body's connective tissues, encourage freedom of movement, and restore a sense of comfort and harmony. Both therapies embrace a holistic approach, honoring the connection between the body, mind, and overall wellness.

What is lymphatic drainage massage?expand_more

Lymphatic drainage is a gentle, rhythmic massage technique designed to encourage the movement of lymphatic fluid. It may be helpful for certain types of swelling, but some medical conditions require clearance before treatment. We're happy to discuss whether this service is appropriate for you.

What are reflexology and Raindrop Technique?expand_more

Reflexology involves applying pressure to specific points on the feet or hands to encourage relaxation and overall well-being. Raindrop Technique combines gentle massage techniques with the application of essential oils for an aromatic wellness experience.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?expand_more

All services are available by appointment only. Simply send us a message with your preferred service and a few dates and times that work best for you. We'll be happy to help coordinate your visit!

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?expand_more

We understand that life happens! We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. This allows us to offer your appointment time to another client. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice are subject to a $25 cancellation fee.

What should I wear to my massage appointment?expand_more

Wear whatever makes you feel comfortable! Depending on your service, you may be asked to undress to your comfort level. Professional draping is used throughout the massage to ensure your privacy and comfort.

Can I request a lighter or firmer pressure during my massage?expand_more

Absolutely! Your massage should always feel comfortable and tailored to you. We encourage open communication throughout your session, so please let us know if you'd prefer more or less pressure or would like any adjustments.

Still Have Questions?

We're always happy to help! Reach out with any questions about our services, scheduling, or finding the right massage for your needs. We look forward to helping you feel your best!
